Cardboard Ski Boot

Made for a Materials Studies and Practice project, we were tasked to make an accurate model shoe using exclusively cardboard and glue. With my interest in the outdoor industry, I created a copy of my ski boot. Featuring working straps and an adjustable tongue, the shoe is full scale and wearable.

All-Night Treasure Hunt

The All-Night Treasure Hunt is an immersive, real-world activity spanning eight hours, from 10pm to 6am. Over this time, a team of 30+ participants work through clues and follow a narrative from location to location, often uncovering an elaborate plot and confronting a villain. The event runs on back-to-back nights so that two teams can compete for the fastest time. Producing it requires roughly two months of planning, physical construction, and coordination of logistics and timing, along with managing a crew of 12+ people. I have been responsible for writing and running three separate events across three years; an experience that has taught me an immense amount about responsibility, team leadership, and problem-solving. It has also allowed for remarkably creative builds and projects. Some of my favorites include:

  • Three unique escape rooms, encompassing both the design of in-room clues and construction of the rooms themselves (one year even featured a hand-dug tunnel system for teams to navigate)
  • Hand-sewn, hand-written, and intentionally weathered story booklets
  • A 3D game board featuring handmade miniature models
  • A puzzle map that illuminated once fully assembled
  • Realistic fake newspaper clippings, archives, and documents
  • Makeup and costume design

RUO: Music Player

Designed in a group for an Arduino and electronics class, RUO is an interactive sound system and decorative statement piece built to bring physicality back to music listening. Its defining feature is a rotating magnetic interface: twisting the bottom-right cylinder uses internal magnets and cams to rotate the screen into view and power the device on. The remaining controls are wired to a hidden Arduino that reads local audio files and communicates with Processing to handle playback.

Boulder Hike Finder

Designed for Computational Foundations 2 final project implementing binary search tree algorithms: Boulder is one of the premiere cities for hiking, with an estimated 170+ hikes in the greater county area alone, making an impressive 300+ miles of trails to explore. For people looking to hike, that number can be overwhelming. Boulder Hike Finder implements Binary Search Trees to rank different attributes, such as elevation, distance, and rating, to help users find the perfect hike fit for them.

Bad Volume Design

Design challenge requiring the creation of an intentionally frustrating volume control interface built in Java. My design requires users drag and drop post it notes to cover a speaker, leaning into the most tedious interaction I could think of. The volume is calculated by measuring how much each square overlaps the speaker, using randomly sized squares to add to the difficulty.

Boulder Problems

All shot on location in Boulder Colorado, bouldering, sport climbing, and high-class scrambling are on display. Along with colorful landscapes and rock formations, as the views and the activities go hand in hand. A grainy effect is present in nearly all the photos, inspired by the early 2000s camera used to capture these photos, and later enhanced in editing to give an old-school REI magazine or Polaroid feel, which also frames a handful of the climbers presented. Designed for a photography midterm to be displayed as a picture book and displayed in ATLAS Expo 2026.
